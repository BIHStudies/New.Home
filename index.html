Welcome to BIHS

Curiosity meets purpose.

The Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies is an international center for research, education, and dialogue at the intersection of science, philosophy, and the Bhāgavata tradition.

Through courses, conferences, and publications, we bring contemporary scholarship and enduring philosophical insights into conversation—exploring questions from consciousness and cosmology to ethics and ecology.

Our Vision

To foster open inquiry that deepens our understanding of reality and encourages wisdom, compassion, and ethical responsibility.

RESEARCH AT BIHS ✦

Five frontiers.
Endless questions.

We explore five fundamental areas of inquiry: cosmogenesis, biogenesis, evolution, consciousness, and metaphysics. Drawing upon the Bhāgavata tradition and contemporary science and philosophy, we encourage rigorous inquiry, open dialogue, and critical examination of ideas.

01 Cosmogenesis How did the universe begin? 02 Biogenesis How did life arise? 03 Evolution How does life change? 04 Consciousness What is it to be aware? 05 Metaphysics What is ultimately real?

Exploring the deepest questions of science, philosophy, and human existence.
